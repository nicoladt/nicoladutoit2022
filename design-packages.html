UX/UI Packages

Timeboxed, priced engagements for short-term work — for teams who know what they need and want to move quickly.

Prices are estimates based on an hourly rate, calculated from a typical hours range per package. Final pricing is confirmed after a short scoping conversation.

Heuristic Evaluation

A structured expert review of your product against established usability principles. Fast, high-signal, and doesn't require access to your users — just a working product and a clear question.

Scope
One core flow or up to 7 key screens (e.g. checkout, onboarding, a dashboard)
Timeline
5 working days from kickoff to delivery
Deliverable
A written report with severity-rated findings, recommendations plus a 60-minute walkthrough call
Investment
R25,000 - R35,000 Up to ~40 hours work

UX/UI Design Sprint

A scoped, timeboxed redesign of a single flow or feature, from problem framing through to implementation-ready designs. For teams who know what's broken but don't have the internal capacity to fix it properly.

Scope
One flow or feature, from problem framing to hi-fidelity Figma designs
Timeline
2 weeks, with a mid-sprint check-in and a final walkthrough
Deliverable
Annotated designs plus a short rationale document your dev team can work from
Investment
R50,000 – R65,000 Up to ~80 hours work

Format
4 × 60-minute virtual or in-person (Johannesburg) sessions over 4–6 weeks, 1:1 or small group of up to 4
Includes
Async WhatsApp or email support between sessions for quick questions
Investment

UX/UI Masterclass or Workshop

Includes

Investment
